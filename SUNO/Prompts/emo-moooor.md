# SUNO V6 TESTING METHODOLOGY

## Phase 1 — Establish the Baseline

Generate Version A using the indicated Style Influence and Weirdness percentages.

Evaluate:

- Vocal clarity
- Drum-pocket consistency
- Low-end weight
- Hook memorability
- Guitar and keyboard balance
- Natural vocal character
- Regional stylistic authenticity

## Phase 2 — Test the Experimental Arrangement

Generate Version B using identical lyrics.

Evaluate:

- Whether unconventional instruments improve the hook.
- Whether sound effects remain behind the lead vocal.
- Whether experimental transitions create useful contrast.
- Whether the arrangement preserves a convincing street-rap identity.
- Whether the vocal remains clear and deliberate.

## Phase 3 — Adjust Weirdness

If Version B introduces unwanted instrumentation, excessive atmospheric effects, or less intelligible vocals, reduce Weirdness by approximately 5–10 percentage points.

If the generated arrangement is too conventional, increase Weirdness gradually while maintaining the same Style Box.

## Phase 4 — Preserve Vocal Consistency

For all concepts, prioritize:

- Deep adult male baritone
- Natural vocal texture
- Crisp consonant articulation
- Deliberate half-time rhythmic phrasing
- Strong pocket awareness
- Clear, intelligible lyrics
- Minimal backing vocals
- Restrained melodic hooks
- Dry, upfront lead vocal

Avoid increasing vocal complexity merely to make an arrangement more experimental.

The experimentation should primarily affect instrumentation, sampling, arrangement, and transitions.

## Core Production Philosophy

**Keep the beat hard, the hook memorable, the vocals intelligible, and the experimental elements restrained.**

The objective is not to make conventional emo trap sound increasingly electronic or cinematic.

It is to combine:

**1990s Memphis underground menace + early-2000s street-rap personality + emotionally charged guitar melodies + modern trap low-end.**

Every concept should remain believable as a gritty independent street record originating from Memphis, Chicago, New York, or Detroit.
