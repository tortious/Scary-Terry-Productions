# SUNO V6 GENERATION WORKFLOW

1. Select Suno V6.
2. Set Variety to OFF.
3. Enable Max Mode for longer productions.
4. Paste the chosen STYLE BOX into the Style field.
5. Paste its EXCLUSIONS into the Exclude Styles field.
6. Set Weirdness and Style Influence to the listed percentages.
7. Add original lyrics with bracketed section descriptors.
8. Generate 4–6 variations.
9. Compare vocal clarity, drum impact, bass definition and cinematic atmosphere.
10. Adjust one parameter at a time.

**Recommended first-generation selections:** 01, 06, 11, 12.

**Core production philosophy:** Hard gangster drums first, cinematic atmosphere second, lo-fi texture third. The instrumental should sound dangerous and expensive while the vocal remains effortlessly clear, relaxed and commanding.
