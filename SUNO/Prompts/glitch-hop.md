# PSYCHEDELIC GLITCH-HOP × DEMONIC TRAP
## 20 Suno V6 Production Blueprints

**Concept:** Catchy, glitchy, psychedelic hip-hop with sinister undertones, unconventional instrumentation, warped melodies, hypnotic rhythms, and varying degrees of aggression, melancholy, arrogance, and experimental insanity.

**Total Presets:** 20  
**Platform:** Suno V6  
**Style Box Limit:** 1,000 characters  
**Exclusion Limit:** 500 characters  
**Vocal Baseline:** Deep male baritone, articulate pronunciation, deliberate cadence, minimal ad-libs, dry upfront lead.

### Global Production Rules

- Prioritize memorable three-to-five-note melodic hooks.
- Use unconventional instruments and psychedelic transformations.
- Maintain heavy, clearly defined bass and percussion.
- Incorporate controlled glitch effects, reverse audio, granular processing, and rhythmic interruptions.
- Preserve a recognizable groove beneath experimental production.
- Keep lead vocals clear and intelligible.
- Avoid speed rap, double-time delivery, whispering, screaming, mumbling, and distorted lead vocals.
- Use deliberate half-time vocal phrasing.
- Balance sinister atmospheres with genuinely catchy melodies.
- Treat Weirdness and Style Influence percentages as recommended starting points.

# FIVE PRIORITY EXPERIMENTS

### 1. Interdimensional Swagger — #08

**Best for:** Confident, punchline-heavy verses with psychedelic production.

Combines rubbery synthesizer hooks, G-funk rhythms, theremin arpeggios, and heavy trap percussion.

### 2. The Devil's Kaleidoscope — #10

**Best for:** Maximum psychedelic intensity without sacrificing rhythmic catchiness.

Features rotating synthesizer patterns, granular pitch spirals, detuned music-box melodies, and explosive bass.

### 3. Psychotic Doo-Wop Glitch — #16

**Best for:** Distinctive genre experimentation and bizarre melodic hooks.

Combines vintage doo-wop harmonies, detuned toy piano, warped organs, glitch effects, and modern Memphis percussion.

### 4. Subconscious Sabotage — #19

**Best for:** Psychological horror, paranoid lyrical themes, and cerebral production.

Uses chromatic electric-piano motifs, reversed tape fragments, mechanical percussion, and unsettling spatial effects.

### 5. Villain's Lullaby — #20

**Best for:** Songs transitioning between melancholy and aggressive confidence.

Begins with fragile music-box melodies and ghostly harmonics before escalating into heavier bass, sinister organs, and forceful percussion.

# GLOBAL VOCAL PRODUCTION STANDARD

Apply these principles across the entire collection:

| Element | Preferred Direction |
|---|---|
| Lead Voice | Deep adult male baritone |
| Diction | Crystal-clear, articulate |
| Cadence | Deliberate, controlled, syncopated |
| Vocal Placement | Dry, upfront, centered |
| Delivery | Confident, expressive, measured |
| Flow | Half-time pocket |
| Ad-Libs | Minimal |
| Vocal Layering | Restrained |
| Vocal Distortion | Avoid on primary lead |
| Instrumental Processing | Experimental, glitchy, psychedelic |
| Bass | Heavy, controlled, prominent |
| Hooks | Memorable, repetitive, unconventional |
| Overall Mix | Clean vocals against strange, evolving instrumentals |

## Core Production Philosophy

**Keep the rapper grounded while the instrumental loses its mind.**

The lead vocal should remain articulate, rhythmically confident, and intelligible even when the surrounding production becomes increasingly psychedelic, glitched, sinister, or unpredictable.

Use instrumental instability as a contrast to vocal control.

**Catchiness is mandatory. Weirdness is intentional. Aggression is controlled. Melancholy is atmospheric.**
