The cleanest, catchiest version of the concept: HOA-core respectability over something that sounds like a RICO exhibit.

• Vibe: Instantly catchy, smug, domestic, brutally straight-faced.
• Notable sounds / instruments: Mailbox clicks, hedge-trimmer pulses, porch knocks, keypad beeps, three-hit doo-wop stab.

Style Box — 997 characters

131 BPM / 65.5 BPM feel, white-picket racketeering trap built for maximum hook retention: simple harmonic DNA, brutal drum clarity and bizarrely respectable surface details. Use upright piano, Rhodes, muted guitar, vibraphone, celesta and a male doo-wop chord sampled into three short rhythmic hits. Keep the progression instantly recognizable after one listen, but shift the bass note under the final chord to create a sinister unresolved loop. Drums are dry and huge: clipped kick, compact snare, sparse hats, rim ghosts and a dense mono 808 with one slow glide before each hook. Add mailbox clicks, porch-step knocks, hedge-trimmer pulses and garage-keypad beeps as nearly subliminal percussion. Male rap is low, articulate, gangsta and brutally quotable, with internal rhyme, homeowner vocabulary twisted into threats and confident dead-air pauses. Hook is a six-word-or-less slogan repeated twice with crew-like harmony responses. Make it catchy, hood, polished and completely straight-faced.
