# 15 SUNO V6 STYLE BOXES

**1950s Sock-Hop × Doo-Wop × Modern Trap**

15 different fusions | Catchy melodies | Unusual instrumentation | Heavy 808s | Creative sound design

Every version combines the innocent, ridiculously catchy melodies of 1950s doo-wop and sock-hop music with modern, hard-hitting trap production.

The variations explore everything from bouncy bubblegum gangsta rap and sinister jukebox melodies to psychedelic doo-wop, ghostly harmonies, distorted carnival instruments, and futuristic rockabilly trap.

## Global production approach

- **Lead vocals:** Deep, confident male baritone; exceptionally clear pronunciation; controlled, rhythmic, articulate rapping.
- **Hooks:** Catchy, memorable melodies with distinctive 1950s doo-wop harmonies.
- **Production:** Heavy 808s, punchy kicks, snappy snares, inventive hi-hats, and interesting instrumental ear candy.
- **Variation:** Different BPMs, melodic contours, chord progressions, rhythmic grooves, and unexpected musical transitions.
- **Vocal separation:** Lead vocals consistently prominent above the instrumental.
- **Limits:** Every Style box is under 1,000 characters; every Exclusions box is under 500 characters.
