# RECOMMENDED TEST ORDER

For your combination of **white suburban-gangsta rap, elaborate multisyllabic rhymes, dark humor, and infectious hooks**, I'd prioritize these five:

### 1. THE CANDY-COATED CONSPIRACY
Closest to your original concept. Sweet doo-wop harmonies, psychedelic Motown instrumentation, funky rhythmic movement, and particularly heavy modern trap production.

### 2. NICKEL-PLATED NURSERY
Best for unusual cadences, deliberate pauses, dense internal rhymes, and unexpected punchlines. The mechanical funk arrangement provides excellent opportunities for rhythmic experimentation.

### 3. VELVET SWITCHBLADE
A balanced combination of vintage charm, gangsta energy, and commercial accessibility.

### 4. RADIOACTIVE ROMANCE
The strongest psychedelic option. Its theremin melodies and acid-funk bass make it particularly suitable for surreal, concept-heavy lyrics.

### 5. THE MILKSHAKE MAFIA
The most energetic and overtly funky. Best when you want an instantly recognizable groove with outrageous lyrical swagger.
