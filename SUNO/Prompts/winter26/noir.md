# SUNO V6 — SMOKE, SWAGGER & BAD INTENTIONS
## THE AFTER-HOURS NOIR EDITION
### 15 Midnight Jazz × Gangster Rap × Smoky Crooner Productions

---

# COLLECTION CONCEPT

A slick, cynical, deep-voiced nightclub crooner with the vocabulary of a gangster, the confidence of a mob boss, and absolutely no moral compass.

Picture an exclusive jazz lounge at 2:30 a.m. Dark mahogany, expensive whiskey, thick cigar smoke hanging beneath amber lights, rain against the windows, and a nearly empty room.

The vocalist alternates between smooth baritone crooning, measured melodic talk-singing, and hard, articulate gangster rap.

The music is sophisticated, nocturnal, hypnotic, melancholic, luxurious, and quietly menacing.

The dark comedy comes from the lyrics, the arrogance, and the vocalist's deadpan delivery—not from cartoonish instrumentation or exaggerated musical theater.

**Core Aesthetic:** Somber music accompanying outrageous, arrogant, darkly comedic lyrics.

---

# REVISED MUSICAL FOUNDATION

| Element | Original Direction | Revised Direction |
|---|---|---|
| Atmosphere | Lively casino entertainment | Empty nightclub at 2:30 a.m. |
| Piano | Bouncy ragtime, honky-tonk | Sparse, dark, resonant jazz piano |
| Bass | Busy walking upright bass | Slow upright plucks, deep electric bass, sub-bass |
| Brass | Jaunty trumpet and trombone | Muted trumpet, smoky tenor sax, low brass |
| Drums | Swinging, snappy, energetic | Dusty boom-bap, brushed jazz, restrained trap |
| Harmony | Bright major chords, playful chromatics | Minor 9ths, minor 11ths, diminished passing chords |
| Melodies | Cheerful, theatrical hooks | Hypnotic, seductive, melancholic motifs |
| Vocals | Exaggerated showman | Smooth, world-weary, confident baritone |
| Humor | Musical comedy | Dry sarcasm, understated arrogance |
| Energy | Upbeat and animated | Relaxed, hypnotic, quietly menacing |

## GLOBAL PRODUCTION RULES

1. Preserve the rap/croon hybrid.
2. Eliminate ragtime, vaudeville, honky-tonk, and cartoonish jazz.
3. Favor minor keys and sophisticated extended jazz harmonies.
4. Use sparse, memorable three- and four-note instrumental motifs.
5. Keep the instrumental arrangements spacious and uncluttered.
6. Emphasize warm analog textures and intimate recording characteristics.
7. Maintain substantial hip-hop percussion and deep bass.
8. Keep the lead vocal dry, prominent, and clearly intelligible.
9. Express dark comedy through lyrical content and deadpan timing.
10. Prioritize atmosphere, sophistication, and effortless swagger over theatrical energy.

## UNIVERSAL VOCAL IDENTITY

**Primary Voice:** Deep masculine baritone.

**Rap Delivery:** Slow, articulate, rhythmically confident, conversational, and occasionally aggressive without shouting.

**Crooning:** Smooth, resonant, low-register, melodic, and restrained.

**Talk-Singing:** Controlled, rhythmically precise, and used primarily for transitions.

**Vocal Character:** Cynical, arrogant, charismatic, sarcastic, world-weary, and quietly dangerous.

### TARGET VOCAL DISTRIBUTION

| Vocal Approach | Approximate Share | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| Rhythmic Rap | 50% | Verses, intricate rhymes, punchlines |
| Melodic Crooning | 30% | Catchy hooks and memorable refrains |
| Rhythmic Talk-Singing | 20% | Transitions, bridges, sarcastic accents |

These percentages are creative targets rather than enforceable Suno settings.

# UNIVERSAL VOCAL ARRANGEMENT

Deep masculine baritone with three distinct vocal modes:

**1. RHYTHMIC RAP**

Slow, articulate, rhythmically precise gangster rap with strong consonant definition, deliberate pauses, confident phrasing and a heavy behind-the-beat pocket.

**2. MELODIC CROONING**

Smooth, rich, low-register crooning with sustained vowels, sophisticated minor-key melodies, restrained vibrato and a smoky vintage vocal character.

**3. MELODIC TALK-SINGING**

Measured melodic phrasing positioned between singing and rapping. Used for transitions, sarcastic declarations and understated dramatic emphasis.

- No whisper-led passages.

A charismatic, deep-voiced crooner delivering outrageous, arrogant and darkly comedic lyrics over genuinely sophisticated midnight jazz, then transitioning seamlessly into hard, articulate gangster rap.
