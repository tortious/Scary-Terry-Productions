## PHONK / MEMPHIS

### 1. Memphis Tape Phonk
```
1990s Memphis underground tape rap, 138 BPM with a dragging halftime backbeat and swung 16th hats. A detuned cowbell carries a three-note loop; the bass is a thin overdriven sub locked to the kick; drums are a distorted 808 kit with a gated snare and audible tape hiss. Low deadpan male vocal, almost spoken, sitting slightly behind the beat, mixed close with a short slap echo. Ad-libs only as a low echoed "yeah" at the end of every fourth bar. Four bars of loop alone, drums drop out for the second half of verse two, everything returns for the last hook. Deliberately lo-fi, narrow mono-leaning image, clipping tape saturation, no modern polish.
```
**Exclude:** clean modern mastering, autotune, sung chorus, glossy digital reverb, trap hi-hat rolls
**Sliders:** v6 · Variety 10 · Weirdness 60 · Style Influence 80 · Max off

Male vocal is shouted-deadpan in a mid register, heavily compressed and doubled a fifth below on the hook; gang chants only in the hook. Structure: filtered 808 intro, drop, verse, chant hook, verse, final drop. Loud, saturated, mono-leaning mix with the 808 as the loudest element and transient-clipped drums.

## How to drive these

- **First pass:** duplicate each box, set Variety = 0, generate two pairs. Confirm the groove and vocal register land before touching anything else.
- **One variable at a time.** If the vocal is too clean, change only the vocal sentence — not the drums, Weirdness, and exclude list together.
- **Exclude is your friend.** Every "no X" removed from the positive prompt is one less competing instruction.
- **Simple Mode A/B.** Paste boxes 6, 13, and 16 into Simple verbatim before assuming Advanced is giving you more.
- **Sibling Mashup first.** If two generations each nail half the brief, mash before rewriting.
- **Max Mode:** off for drafts, on only once a box is consistently working.
- **Late-song drift:** the era boxes (11–15) benefit most from the "stable vocal tone and loudness across the entire track" clause — add it if rasp or density creeps in after the second hook.
