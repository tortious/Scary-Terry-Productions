### Quick Usage Tip for "Trippy" Beats + Dry Vocals
Notice that in prompts like **#2 (tape warble)**, **#6 (phaser guitar)**, and **#10 (dub tape delay)**, the psychedelic effects are strictly assigned to the **instruments**, while the vocal remains anchored to `dry close-miked lead vocal, minimal vocal reverb`. This keeps the beat swirling and hypnotic without letting modulation or echo smear your consonants.
