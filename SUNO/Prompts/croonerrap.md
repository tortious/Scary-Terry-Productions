# SUNO V6 — SMOKE, SWAGGER & BAD INTENTIONS

### 15 Snazzy, Smoky, Upbeat, Darkly Comedic Crooner-Rap Productions

**The Concept:** A slick, wisecracking lounge singer with the vocabulary of a gangster, the confidence of a mob boss, and absolutely no moral compass.

Picture a dimly lit 1950s supper club, a crooked Vegas casino, or a smoky jazz lounge—except the singer alternates between smooth baritone crooning, rhythmic talk-singing, and hard, articulate rap verses.

The music should be genuinely catchy, mischievous, sophisticated, and upbeat, with darkly comedic undertones.

## MUSICAL FOUNDATION

We're combining five distinct musical traditions:

1. **1950s–1960s Crooner Jazz:** Swinging piano, upright bass, brushed drums, muted brass.
2. **Vintage Lounge and Nightclub Music:** Hammond organ, vibraphone, finger snaps, walking basslines.
3. **Boom-Bap and Southern Hip-Hop:** Punchy snares, substantial low end, head-nodding grooves.
4. **Vaudeville, Doo-Wop, and Cabaret:** Theatrical arrangements, call-and-response, memorable hooks.
5. **Dark Comedy:** Playful, slightly crooked melodies accompanying outrageous, arrogant lyrics.

### IMPORTANT VOCAL DISTINCTION

Unlike strictly rapped productions, these intentionally feature both rapping and singing.

**Ideal Vocal Progression:**

Deep masculine baritone → conversational rhythmic rap → playful melodic talk-singing → silky crooned hook → dry, articulate rap.

**Avoid:** Delicate pop vocals, excessive falsetto, theatrical screaming, frantic speed-rapping, whispering, mumbling, and distorted lead vocals.
