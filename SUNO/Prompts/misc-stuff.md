# Creepy Sounds

## 1. Creepy instruments, noises & production sounds

### 01. Waterphone / ocean harp
- **Sound:** Unstable metallic moans, glissandi and ringing dissonance; almost a horror-film signature.
- **Where in your songs:** Use an isolated swell before the first downbeat or an 8-bar verse-to-hook change.
- **Execution / mix:** Low-pass the attack, reverse selected tails, keep it high-mid and mono-compatible; never mask the baritone.
- **Suno-ready prompt phrase:** `bowed waterphone tension swell, sparse and distant`
- **Listen:** [Play:  Waterphone Demo: Waters-Hubbert](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=foSJstDFDfg) **(direct video)**

### 02. Bowed cymbal / bowed ride
- **Sound:** A high, glassy scrape that blooms into a sustained metallic whistle.
- **Where in your songs:** Set a 1-2-second rise into hook entrances, then cut to silence at beat one.
- **Execution / mix:** Automate a band-pass filter upward; high-pass to leave kick/808 alone.
- **Suno-ready prompt phrase:** `bowed ride cymbal spectral riser, abrupt hard cut`
- **Listen:** [Play:  Bowed ride-cymbal demonstration](https://youtu.be/t1JhspzrNog) **(direct video)**

### 03. Musical saw
- **Sound:** Singing, wavering, human-like pitch slides that feel like a haunted voice.
- **Where in your songs:** Countermelody in verse gaps; a single falling note after an ominous punchline.
- **Execution / mix:** Sample one note, subtly wow/flutter it, then add short slap delay.
- **Suno-ready prompt phrase:** `ghostly bowed musical saw answering phrases`
- **Listen:** [Play:  Musical Saw  -  instrument demonstration](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=1-4guumjeDU) **(direct video)**

### 04. Prepared piano
- **Sound:** A real piano altered with objects for woody clicks, muted thumps and metallic twangs.
- **Where in your songs:** Dry, asymmetric 2-note motif beneath the verse; leave one rest each measure.
- **Execution / mix:** Layer low plucks with a normal piano only at the hook; control sharp transients.
- **Suno-ready prompt phrase:** `uneven prepared piano knocks, sinister two-note ostinato`
- **Listen:** [Play:  John Cage prepared-piano demonstration](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Ap3NbeLWogI) **(direct video)**

### 05. Spring-reverb tank crash
- **Sound:** A physical spring makes thunderous boings, sproings and dubby decays when struck.
- **Where in your songs:** One transition accent after a snare fill, or a reverb throw at the end of a bar.
- **Execution / mix:** Record/resample just the wet signal; gate decay so it does not wash into lyrics.
- **Suno-ready prompt phrase:** `metallic spring tank crash, short gated dub tail`
- **Listen:** [Play:  Spring Reverb Demonstration](https://youtu.be/tU7U-U-n4EQ) **(direct video)**

### 06. Daxophone
- **Sound:** Bowed wooden tongues produce rasps, squeals and uncannily speech-like sounds.
- **Where in your songs:** Short monster-like answers between bars, not throughout every line.
- **Execution / mix:** Pitch-shift down a few semitones and chop into call-and-response fragments.
- **Suno-ready prompt phrase:** `daxophone creature-like wood friction squeals`
- **Listen:** [Play:  Daxophone  -  instrument demonstration](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PyiYoyl2rgQ) **(direct video)**

### 07. Apprehension Engine
- **Sound:** A bespoke friction-and-resonance noise instrument designed for unsettling horror textures.
- **Where in your songs:** Opening 4 seconds, mid-song bridge, or beatless fake-out before a final verse.
- **Execution / mix:** Choose ONE sound (scrape, ratchet, drone); limit the duration and remove 200-400 Hz mud.
- **Suno-ready prompt phrase:** `apprehension engine friction scrape, minimalist dread`
- **Listen:** [Hear the Sounds of the Nightmare Machine - Great Big Story](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ZmmW5i4nSQ4) **(direct video)**

### 08. Bass-clarinet multiphonics
- **Sound:** A performer generates rough simultaneous pitches, growls and split-tone clusters.
- **Where in your songs:** A one-shot horror stab at the end of a 4-bar phrase.
- **Execution / mix:** Short envelope, mildly saturated; duck 250-800 Hz under lead vocal.
- **Suno-ready prompt phrase:** `low bass clarinet multiphonic growl, dry dissonant stab`
- **Listen:** [Play:  20 Bass Clarinet Multiphonics](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=R_WPp_8xbnI) **(direct video)**

### 09. Theremin / unstable sine glide
- **Sound:** An untouchable electronic instrument with continuous portamento and vocal-like warble.
- **Where in your songs:** Two sustained notes in a breakdown or a lead-in to a spooky hook.
- **Execution / mix:** Keep vibrato slow, low in the mix; pitch to key if using a sampler.
- **Suno-ready prompt phrase:** `detuned theremin portamento, controlled eerie vibrato`
- **Listen:** [Play:  The THEREMIN  -  instrument demonstration](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=m5MzV1cGd-I) **(direct video)**

### 10. Glass armonica / rubbing glass
- **Sound:** Very pure, shivering glass harmonics with a fragile floating quality.
- **Where in your songs:** Background high-register harmonics behind an otherwise dirty 808 groove.
- **Execution / mix:** Modulate stereo width slowly; roll off harsh top end if stacking bells.
- **Suno-ready prompt phrase:** `glass armonica ghost harmonics, delicate glassy sustain`
- **Listen:** [Play:  The Glass Armonica  -  Rob Scallon](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=21qItSMlrWQ) **(direct video)**

### 11. Bowed electric guitar
- **Sound:** A cello bow across guitar strings produces huge friction swells and feedback-like clouds.
- **Where in your songs:** Held single-note bed under intros, then remove it when rap begins.
- **Execution / mix:** Stack gentle distortion and long reverb *on the background return*; filter below 250 Hz.
- **Suno-ready prompt phrase:** `bowed electric guitar drone, foggy resonant swell`
- **Listen:** [Play:  Live performance featuring bowed guitar](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lXMhOixwyew) **(direct video)**

### 12. Contact-mic metal scrape
- **Sound:** Close-miked sheet metal, wire or a fence makes exaggerated tiny grinds and shrieks.
- **Where in your songs:** Single offbeat punctuation before a snare; use like percussion rather than a pad.
- **Execution / mix:** Stretch to reveal harmonics; carve harsh 3-5 kHz and pitch down.
- **Suno-ready prompt phrase:** `close-mic metal scrape, granular rattling transition`
- **Listen:** [Play:  Browse demonstrations](https://www.youtube.com/results?search_query=contact+microphone+metal+sheet+horror+sound+design+demonstration) **(YouTube search)**

### 13. Reversed tape-piano strike
- **Sound:** A recorded piano note run backward rises unnaturally into a hit.
- **Where in your songs:** Pickup on the last beat before each hook, ending exactly on the downbeat.
- **Execution / mix:** Print reverse reverb/tape wobble; shorten the tail so it does not compete with lyrics.
- **Suno-ready prompt phrase:** `reverse tape piano inhale into isolated minor chord`
- **Listen:** [Play:  Browse demonstrations](https://www.youtube.com/results?search_query=reverse+piano+reverb+swell+music+production+tutorial) **(YouTube search)**

### 14. Creaking door / hinge foley
- **Sound:** A squeak with an almost pitched, breathing contour; mundane but uncanny when isolated.
- **Where in your songs:** Subtle cold-open cue or a rhythmic squeak repeated only every fourth bar.
- **Execution / mix:** Record your own hinge, tune to song, remove sub-rumble, leave dry and close.
- **Suno-ready prompt phrase:** `close-miked creaking hinge, unnerving rhythmic squeak`
- **Listen:** [Play:  Browse demonstrations](https://www.youtube.com/results?search_query=door+hinge+creak+foley+sound+design+recording) **(YouTube search)**

### 15. Ring-modulated voice / radio squall
- **Sound:** A voice or oscillator ring-modulated into metallic sidebands and alien radio tones.
- **Where in your songs:** A split-second pre-hook transmission or whispered-sounding *effect*, not a full verse.
- **Execution / mix:** Process an ad-lib only; band-limit and duck behind the actual lyrics.
- **Suno-ready prompt phrase:** `brief ring-modulated radio voice burst, metallic static`
- **Listen:** [Play:  Browse demonstrations](https://www.youtube.com/results?search_query=ring+modulation+voice+sound+design+demonstration) **(YouTube search)**
