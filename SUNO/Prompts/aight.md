# HOOD-HELPER — 36 SUNO V6 STYLE BOXES

## 12 Distinct Production Styles × 3 Variations Each

These 36 production concepts draw from your Hood-Helper Master Sound Design Reference, incorporating its catalog of unusual instruments, experimental effects, psychedelic transformations, and vocal approaches.

The goal is to create 12 genuinely different musical identities, each with three variations that explore different instrumentation, rhythmic arrangements, and atmospheres—not merely minor changes to the same beat.

The reference specifically prioritizes distinctive instrumental combinations, memorable hooks, and clear lead vocals. `Sound-Design-Resource.txt`

## Universal Settings

All 36 variations follow these standards:

* **Style Box:** Maximum 1,000 characters.
* **Exclusions:** Maximum 500 characters, comma-separated.
* **Vocals:** Deep male baritone, intelligible diction, controlled delivery.
* **Cadence:** Deliberate, rhythmic, and generally slow-to-moderate.
* **Production:** Strong bass, memorable instrumental motifs, clean vocal separation.
* **Excluded:** Speed rap, double-time, mumbling, whispering, screaming, distorted lead vocals.
* **Settings:** Each includes suggested Style Influence and Weirdness percentages.

The vocal defaults also follow your project's established performance preferences. `Custom-Project-Instructions.txt`
