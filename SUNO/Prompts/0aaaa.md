# THE BLACK RITUAL COLLECTION

**18 Original Cinematic Horror-Trap Productions — 36 Suno V6 Variations**

Production direction: Three 6 Mafia's sinister Memphis atmosphere, Shyne's commanding vocal weight, and Bump J's cold Chicago delivery—reinterpreted through original arrangements, experimental sound design, and cinematic horror.

These 18 concepts each have two independently developed versions, giving you 36 production-ready combinations of style boxes, exclusions, and recommended Suno settings.

## Production Standards

Every production follows these requirements:

- **Vocals:** Deep, authoritative, intelligible, precisely articulated. Never screaming, whispering, mumbling, or unnecessarily fast.
- **Flow:** Flexible bar lengths, multisyllabic rhyme chains, internal rhymes, displaced accents, unconventional schemes, and deliberate pauses.
- **Effects:** Heavy creative use of reverse reverb, glitch delays, vinyl scratching, turntable manipulation, pitch-shifted vocal shadows, and tape effects.
- **Clarity:** Aggressive effects occur around the vocals, not across entire verses. Every punchline remains understandable.
- **Cinematic character:** Distinctive instrumentation, unsettling atmospheres, tension, and controlled dynamic development.
- **Multiple performers:** Clearly distinguishable voices, individually assigned verses, and intentional transitions.
- **Arrangement:** Lyrics control pacing. Instrumental changes support the writing rather than forcing conventional song structures.

Each Style box is designed for Suno's 1,000-character field, with a separate exclusion list under 500 characters. Percentages refer to the Style Influence and Weirdness controls. Sound-Design-Resource.txt

## PRODUCTION NOTES — MAKING ALL 36 WORK WITH COMPLEX LYRICS

### 1. Multisyllabic and unconventional rhymes

The style descriptions deliberately avoid requiring every phrase to fit a uniform four-beat delivery.

These productions are designed to accommodate:

- **Extended multisyllabic chains:** Three-, four-, five-, and six-syllable rhyme patterns without forcing rapid delivery.
- **Mosaic rhymes:** Multiple shorter words matching the sounds of longer phrases.
- **Displaced rhyme resolution:** Rhymes arriving before the downbeat, after the expected ending, or in the following measure.
- **Enjambment:** Lyrical phrases continuing across bar lines without forced pauses.
- **Unusual rhyme structures:** ABCB, ABCA, internal-to-terminal chains, deliberately broken patterns, and changing rhyme families.
- **Stop-start cadence:** Long phrases followed by deliberate silence to emphasize a punchline.

The instrumental pocket should remain stable while the rapper's phrasing moves around it.

### 2. Deep-pitched vocal effects

A critical distinction: the primary lead vocal should not be excessively pitch-shifted.

The intended effect is a clearly intelligible baritone supported by selectively manipulated secondary layers.

| Processing | Recommended application |
|---|---|
| Octave-down shadow | Selected final words and emphasized phrases |
| Chopped-and-screwed doubles | Outro phrases, transitions and isolated hook responses |
| Reverse reverb | Swells immediately preceding significant words |
| Granular glitch delay | Short phrase endings, after the original words |
| Pitch-dropping echo | Final syllables and dramatic punchlines |
| Ring-modulated vocal fragments | Background texture or transitions |
| Formant-shifted doubles | Subtle character contrast in selected passages |
| Tape-stop vocal tails | Dramatic cuts immediately following a complete phrase |
