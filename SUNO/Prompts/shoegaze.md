# SHOEGAZE × GANGSTA TRAP
## 15 Original Suno V6 Production Concepts

**Deep Baritone • Methodical Rap • Cold Delivery • Heavy 808s • Dreamlike Atmospheres**

---

## COLLECTION OVERVIEW

Fifteen original productions combining the intimidating, bass-heavy character of gangsta trap with the immersive, atmospheric qualities of shoegaze.

**Production objective:** Hard gangsta rap surrounded by beautiful, hazy, hypnotic instrumentation—not soft emo rap with trap drums.

### Global Vocal Requirements

- Deep masculine baritone or bass voice
- Cold, detached, menacing delivery
- Slow, methodical rhythmic rapping
- Deliberate pauses and calculated phrasing
- Crisp consonants and intelligible pronunciation
- Restrained aggression
- Dry, upfront lead vocals
- Single lead vocalist
- Minimal ad-libs
- Strictly rapped delivery, not singing

### Global Instrumental Requirements

- Heavy, clean 808 sub-bass
- Hard trap percussion
- Shoegaze electric guitar textures
- Dreamy minor-key harmonies
- Atmospheric reverb and reverse-guitar effects
- Wide stereo instrumentation
- Strong separation between lead vocals and atmospheric layers
- Controlled low-frequency response

### Absolutely Excluded

- Rubbery synthesizer bass
- Fart-like bass sounds
- Squelchy electronic sounds
- Funky basslines
- Slap bass
- Wah-wah guitar
- Comical electronic effects
- Speed rap
- Double-time rap
- Mumble rap
- Whispering
- Screaming
- Distorted lead vocals

# MIXING & SOUND DESIGN PRINCIPLES

## Vocal Placement

The primary rapper must remain:

- Dry
- Centered
- Clearly audible
- Deep and authoritative
- Unaffected by the surrounding shoegaze ambience

The atmospheric quality should come primarily from the instrumentation rather than vocal processing.

## Guitar Placement

Shoegaze guitars should:

- Occupy wide stereo space
- Utilize long diffuse reverb tails
- Feature sustained minor-key harmonies
- Use controlled feedback and distortion
- Avoid masking the vocal frequencies
- Create immersion without sacrificing rhythmic clarity

## Bass Design

Prioritize:

- Clean sine-based 808 sub-bass
- Strong fundamental frequencies
- Controlled low-end sustain
- Precise kick and bass separation
- Restrained pitch movement
- Powerful sub-frequency pressure

Avoid:

- Rubber-like oscillator movement
- Excessive resonant filtering
- Comical pitch modulation
- Squelchy synth bass
- Fart-like bass effects
- Funky slap-bass characteristics

## Drum Design

Maintain:

- Heavy trap kicks
- Sharp snares
- Minimal hi-hats
- Deliberate rhythmic spaces
- Strong half-time emphasis
- Occasional strategic drum dropouts

## Overall Creative Direction

**The ideal contrast:**

An absolutely cold, deep-voiced gangsta rapper delivering calculated, intimidating bars over beautiful, overwhelming, dreamlike atmospheric instrumentation.

The result should sound simultaneously:

- Menacing
- Hypnotic
- Cinematic
- Dreamlike
- Emotionally detached
- Heavy
- Spacious
- Dark
- Controlled

**Core philosophy: Beautiful atmosphere. Brutal rhythm. Ice-cold delivery.**
