# RECOMMENDED SUNO V6 PRODUCTION STRATEGY

## 1. Instrumental Hooks First

Prioritize immediately recognizable melodic motifs.

**Ideal characteristics:**
- Three-to-five-note synthesizer melodies
- Repetitive two-bar keyboard patterns
- Catchy melodic call-and-response
- Occasional pitch bends
- Simple melodies with subtle variations
- Strong contrast between instrumental hooks and vocal phrasing

## 2. Heavy but Controlled 808s

The bass should provide rhythmic movement without overwhelming the melody.

**Preferred characteristics:**
- Thick rounded sub-bass
- Punchy kick and 808 separation
- Short bass phrases
- Occasional controlled slides
- Half-time rhythmic emphasis
- Minimal unnecessary bass distortion

## 3. Deep Baritone Vocal Identity

Maintain a consistent, recognizable lead vocal.

**Preferred characteristics:**
- Deep masculine baritone
- Crisp pronunciation
- Relaxed rhythmic confidence
- Slightly behind-the-beat phrasing
- Deliberate pauses
- Minimal ad-libs
- Dry, upfront lead vocals
- Occasional melodic inflections

## 4. Catchy Hooks Without Excessive Singing

The melodic emphasis should come primarily from the instrumental arrangement and chorus.

**Preferred hook characteristics:**
- Repeated short phrases
- Small melodic pitch movements
- Rhythmic chant-singing
- Minimal harmony doubles
- Memorable cadence patterns
- Controlled vowel extensions
- Consistent vocal identity

## 5. Clean Commercial Mixing

Prioritize clarity, impact, and replay value.

**Mixing objectives:**
- Deep controlled bass
- Bright but smooth synthesizers
- Punchy trap percussion
- Wide stereo instrumental textures
- Centered lead vocals
- Restrained reverb
- Short delays on selected hook endings
- Minimal competing melodies

# MASTER PRODUCTION FORMULA

**Genre:** Catchy melodic trap × atmospheric pop-rap × futuristic Southern trap

**Tempo:** 130–152 BPM

**Half-Time Feel:** 65–76 BPM

**Primary Melody:** Repetitive three-to-five-note synthesizer or keyboard hook

**Secondary Texture:** Rhodes, celesta, marimba, vibraphone, analog pads, or guitar harmonics

**Bass:** Thick rounded 808 with strong rhythmic bounce

**Drums:** Punchy kick, oversized clap-snare, crisp restrained hi-hats

**Vocals:** Deep articulate male baritone, relaxed confident delivery

**Chorus:** Catchy rhythmic chant with subtle melodic movement

**Mix:** Clean, polished, spacious, bass-heavy, radio-ready

**Overall Character:** Luxurious, playful, hypnotic, melodic, charismatic, and extremely catchy.

# FINAL CREATIVE OBJECTIVE

Create infectious melodic trap records featuring:

1. Instantly recognizable keyboard melodies.
2. Powerful Southern trap bass.
3. Simple but addictive rhythmic arrangements.
4. Smooth atmospheric pop-rap production.
5. Subtle psychedelic synthesizer textures.
6. Confident, articulate low-register rap vocals.
7. Catchy choruses without excessive melodic singing.
8. Distinctive instrumental identities.
9. Clean commercial mixing.
10. High replay value.

**Target sound:** An instantly recognizable synthesizer melody, enormous 808 bounce, addictive chorus, spacious professional production, and a distinctive controlled rap performance.

**Core principle:** Keep the beats melodic, the bass heavy, the hooks infectious, and the verses unmistakably rap.
