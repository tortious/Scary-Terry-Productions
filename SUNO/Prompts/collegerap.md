# PRODUCTION OBJECTIVE

Recreate the musical character of late-2000s and early-2010s college-mixtape rap, emphasizing:

- Laid-back, singable acoustic-and-keyboard grooves
- Straight, uncomplicated hip-hop drums
- Warm melodic bass
- Repetitive, catchy chord progressions
- Conversational, humorous male rap delivery
- Memorable communal hooks
- Low-budget independent-mixtape charm
- Natural vocal performances
- Relaxed rhythmic phrasing
- Minimal modern trap influence

The production should prioritize **catchiness, personality, humor, and rhythmic bounce** over technical complexity or aggressive instrumentation.

# PRODUCTION PRIORITIES

## Highest Priority

1. Memorable instrumental groove
2. Natural conversational rap cadence
3. Clear, intelligible lead vocals
4. Catchy melodic chorus
5. Authentic 2008–2012 mixtape atmosphere

## Secondary Priority

1. Warm analog coloration
2. Subtle instrumental imperfections
3. Playful rhythmic syncopation
4. Simple harmonic progressions
5. Distinctive acoustic and keyboard textures

## Elements to Minimize

1. Modern trap production
2. Excessive sub-bass
3. Complicated drum programming
4. Heavy vocal processing
5. Aggressive vocal delivery
6. Dense instrumental layering
7. Overly polished contemporary pop production

# FINAL PRODUCTION TARGET

**A catchy, humorous, laid-back college-rap record that sounds like it could have circulated through dorm rooms, house parties, college radio, and independent mixtape blogs between 2008 and 2012.**

The defining musical characteristics are:

**Acoustic guitar + warm piano + simple hip-hop drums + melodic bass + conversational male rap + memorable communal hooks.**

The overall result should feel:

**Effortless, nostalgic, playful, catchy, rhythmic, and authentically independent.**
