## Slider Adjustments

### More Commercial / Catchier

- Reduce Weirdness by 5–10%.
- Maintain high Style Influence.
- Favor simple melodic motifs.
- Preserve conventional verse-to-chorus transitions.
- Emphasize repetitive, easily remembered hooks.

### More Experimental / Unusual

- Increase Weirdness by 5–10%.
- Preserve the original Style Box.
- Introduce unexpected melodic textures.
- Experiment with unusual instrumental transitions.
- Maintain a strong rhythmic foundation.

### More Aggressive / Harder Trap

- Increase drum transient emphasis.
- Strengthen kick and 808 interaction.
- Reduce melodic density during verses.
- Add controlled saturation to bass instruments.
- Preserve clean, intelligible lead vocals.

### More Pop-Oriented

- Increase melodic repetition.
- Introduce brighter harmonic voicings.
- Use shorter instrumental introductions.
- Expand the chorus instrumentation.
- Favor simple, singable melodic contours.

**Hard trap drums. Catchy pop melodies. Deep articulate rap vocals.**
