# PRODUCTION NOTES

## Core Principle: Simplicity + Repetition = Catchiness

The original works largely because of its simplicity and repetition.

### Melodies

- Keep melodic loops extremely short.
- Prioritize two-note, three-note, and four-note patterns.
- Use cheap, memorable keyboard tones.
- Avoid complicated chord progressions.
- Let repetitive melodies become hypnotic.

### Drums

- Keep kicks and snares prominent.
- Use strong, simple rhythmic patterns.
- Maintain a heavy half-time pocket.
- Avoid excessive percussion fills.
- Use strategic silence for dramatic rhythmic impact.

### Bass

- Favor short, punchy bass notes.
- Keep sub-bass heavy but controlled.
- Avoid excessive 808 slides.
- Allow the bass to reinforce the kick pattern.
- Preserve space between low-end hits.

### Vocals

- Deep, natural male baritone.
- Single dominant lead.
- Crisp consonants and exceptional intelligibility.
- Relaxed Southern rhythmic swagger.
- Deliberate pauses between punchlines.
- Dry, upfront vocal mixing.
- Minimal effects or vocal layering.
- Confident, deadpan comedic delivery.

### Arrangement

- Keep instrumental layers sparse.
- Use repetition as a deliberate production technique.
- Avoid unnecessary melodic flourishes.
- Let rhythm and delivery carry the song.
- Favor memorable hooks over complicated arrangements.

> **Production Rule:** Keep the melodic loops short, the drums prominent, and the vocals dry and intelligible. Resist filling every available space with instrumental flourishes.
