# 36 SUNO V6 PRODUCTION CONCEPTS

**COCKY × LO-FI CHILL × CINEMATIC TRAP × DARK COMEDY**

18 original concepts · 2 production versions each · 36 Style Boxes + 36 Exclusion Lists

## Overall sonic direction

Think Three 6 Mafia's hypnotic Southern low end, Shyne's commanding vocal presence, and Bump J's unhurried Chicago confidence, reimagined through dusty lo-fi production, unconventional instrumentation, understated arrogance, and absurdist humor.

The objective is imposing without sounding angry, funny without becoming goofy, and experimental without sacrificing lyrical clarity.

I've replaced the supernatural horror atmosphere with something more distinctive: a self-important, vaguely ridiculous villain who sounds completely unbothered by everything.

All 36 arrangements follow your project's Suno V6 limits and clear-vocal production priorities.

**Production rules across the collection:**

- Deep, articulate male baritone leads; selected tracks introduce contrasting male rappers or female vocalists.
- Slow, controlled flows that accommodate multisyllabic chains, internal rhymes, mosaic rhymes, and unconventional bar lengths.
- Glitch reverb, reverse-reverb anticipation, record scratching, and pitched-down vocal fragments.
- Minimal ad-libs and plenty of negative space around punchlines.
- Lo-fi coloration on instruments, not unintelligible lead vocals.
- Some productions maintain an unwavering hypnotic groove; others build toward controlled cinematic crescendos.
- Comedic timing comes primarily from the lyrics, delivery, and unexpected instrumental punctuation.

**A versions** generally emphasize restrained, hypnotic minimalism.

**B versions** explore alternative arrangements, unusual sound design, contrasting vocalists, or controlled intensity builds.

*The percentages are suggested Style Influence / Weirdness settings, not text to paste into Suno.*

**72 BPM | Consistently slow | Influence 78% | Weirdness 24%**
