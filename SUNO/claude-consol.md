# Scary Terry Productions — Sound & Instrument Reference

A working reference for the cinematic-horror-trap / boom-bap / jazz-rap sound palette: instruments, foley, vocal FX, and production textures, with how to use each one and where to hear it.

> **Note on "real songs" column:** for the well-documented instruments below, examples are confirmed. For niche/experimental instruments that are rare or unconfirmed in mainstream rap, that's noted honestly rather than guessed — most of those come from film scoring, and using them in rap is exactly what would make the catalog distinctive.

## 1. Cinematic / Horror Instruments

### Waterphone
- **Sound:** Metallic groans, whale-like shrieks, ghostly pitch bends
- **Vibe:** Dread, psychological horror, stalking-prey tension
- **Use in a rap track:** First 10 seconds of an intro, right before the 808 enters, at a beat switch, or reversed into a snare hit
- **Real usage:** A horror-film-score staple (heard across countless thrillers/horror soundtracks); not a standard hip-hop element — bringing it into rap is a genuine point of difference
- **Video:** [Waterphone demo search](https://www.youtube.com/results?search_query=waterphone+demo+horror+sound)

### Bassoon
- **Sound:** Low, woody, nasal — like a sarcastic villain laughing
- **Vibe:** Calculating menace, mafia strategist, intellectual dread
- **Use in a rap track:** Lead or counter-melody, call-and-response with piano, short dry stabs right before the 808 hits
- **Real usage:** Orchestral/classical (e.g., the famous bassoon solo opening Stravinsky's *The Rite of Spring*); not a hip-hop staple, which is the appeal
- **Video:** [Bassoon solo — Rite of Spring](https://www.youtube.com/results?search_query=bassoon+solo+rite+of+spring)

### Contrabass Clarinet
- **Sound:** Deeper and creepier than a standard clarinet
- **Vibe:** Villain-theme menace
- **Use in a rap track:** Repeating 2–4 note hook or low drone under a hook
- **Real usage:** Mostly orchestral/experimental scoring; no confirmed mainstream rap use
- **Video:** [Contrabass clarinet demo search](https://www.youtube.com/results?search_query=contrabass+clarinet+demo)

### Tubular Bells
- **Sound:** Massive, resonant, cathedral-like bells
- **Vibe:** Apocalypse, judgment, finality
- **Use in a rap track:** One bell hit every 8 bars to accent an important lyric — never overuse
- **Real usage:** Famous in film (Mike Oldfield's *Tubular Bells* is the theme to *The Exorcist*); horror-trap and Memphis-revival producers use them for the same dread effect
- **Video:** [Tubular bells sound demo](https://www.youtube.com/results?search_query=tubular+bells+sound+demo)

### Prepared Piano
- **Sound:** Objects placed on/between the strings turn the piano into bells, percussion, and broken machinery
- **Vibe:** Controlled chaos, unsettling, experimental
- **Use in a rap track:** Main melody source for glitch beats, or single hits used like gunmetal percussion
- **Real usage:** Pioneered by composer John Cage in concert music; essentially unused in mainstream rap — a genuinely rare texture to bring in
- **Video:** [John Cage prepared piano demo](https://www.youtube.com/results?search_query=john+cage+prepared+piano+demonstration)

### Glass Harmonica
- **Sound:** Rotating glass bowls rubbed with wet fingers — impossibly smooth, ghostly tone
- **Vibe:** Haunted mansion, beautiful insanity, luxury horror
- **Use in a rap track:** Ambient hook pad, layered under piano, reversed into transitions
- **Real usage:** Invented/popularized by Benjamin Franklin; Mozart wrote for it (*Adagio for Glass Harmonica*, K. 356); no known mainstream rap example
- **Video:** [Glass harmonica — Mozart Adagio](https://www.youtube.com/results?search_query=glass+harmonica+mozart+adagio)

### Cristal Baschet
- **Sound:** Glass rods rubbed and amplified through metal cones/resonators
- **Vibe:** Alien, floating, psychedelic
- **Use in a rap track:** Ethereal melody line under sparse, spacious trap drums
- **Real usage:** A modern (1950s) sculptural instrument, used mostly in film/experimental scoring; not established in rap
- **Video:** [Cristal Baschet demo](https://www.youtube.com/results?search_query=cristal+baschet+demonstration)

### Bowed Vibraphone
- **Sound:** Vibraphone bars bowed instead of struck — shimmering, endlessly sustained
- **Vibe:** Frozen air, crystal caves, spiritual
- **Use in a rap track:** Background texture layered behind vocals
- **Real usage:** Common in contemporary classical/film scoring; not a hip-hop staple
- **Video:** [Bowed vibraphone demo](https://www.youtube.com/results?search_query=bowed+vibraphone+demonstration)

### Church Organ (low stops)
- **Sound:** Deep, funeral-drill, gothic minor chords
- **Vibe:** Gothic intimidation, "demonic Memphis" energy
- **Use in a rap track:** Hold a minor chord under the hook
- **Real usage:** A recurring texture in Memphis-revival/"phonk" and horrorcore-leaning trap; church organ stabs are common in that specific underground lane
- **Video:** [Dark church organ trap sound](https://www.youtube.com/results?search_query=dark+church+organ+trap+beat)

## 2. World & Ethnic Instruments

### Duduk
- **Sound:** Breathy Armenian double-reed woodwind, almost like a person quietly singing through wood
- **Vibe:** Lonely, ancient, mournful, mafia/desert menace
- **Use in a rap track:** Long bent notes with reverb over sparse drums; ideal under a somber storytelling verse
- **Real usage:** Widely used in film scoring (Djivan Gasparyan's playing appears in *Gladiator* and *Blood Diamond*); not established in hip-hop, so it's a genuine differentiator
- **Video:** [Djivan Gasparyan — Duduk](https://www.youtube.com/results?search_query=djivan+gasparyan+duduk)

### Koto
- **Sound:** Japanese plucked zither — crisp, cold, precise plucks with atmosphere
- **Vibe:** Surgical, stealth, exotic luxury
- **Use in a rap track:** Short riffs every 4 bars, especially right before a punchline
- **Real usage:** **RZA has used koto directly in Wu-Tang-affiliated production** — his track "Koto Chotan" (with Masta Killa & Tash Mahogany) is built around koto and takes its name from the instrument; Wu-Tang's catalog broadly draws on East-Asian instrumentation as part of its kung-fu-film aesthetic
- **Video:** [Koto solo performance](https://www.youtube.com/results?search_query=koto+solo+performance)

### Erhu
- **Sound:** Chinese two-string bowed fiddle — one of the closest instruments to the human voice
- **Vibe:** Regret, isolation, cinematic sorrow
- **Use in a rap track:** Emotional solo or bridge melody
- **Real usage:** Common in Chinese film scoring; no confirmed major rap placement, though used occasionally as a sample source in lo-fi/beat-tape circles
- **Video:** [Erhu solo performance](https://www.youtube.com/results?search_query=erhu+solo+performance)

### Sarangi
- **Sound:** Rich, human-voice-like Indian bowed instrument
- **Vibe:** Haunted, intimate, human
- **Use in a rap track:** Vocal-like countermelody
- **Real usage:** Core to Hindustani classical music; not established in rap
- **Video:** [Sarangi solo demo](https://www.youtube.com/results?search_query=sarangi+solo+demonstration)

### Nyckelharpa
- **Sound:** Swedish keyed fiddle with sympathetic strings that resonate on their own
- **Vibe:** Viking noir, ancient mystery, cold forests
- **Use in a rap track:** Dark melodic loop
- **Real usage:** Traditional Scandinavian folk instrument; no rap precedent
- **Video:** [Nyckelharpa demo](https://www.youtube.com/results?search_query=nyckelharpa+demonstration)

### Hardanger Fiddle
- **Sound:** Norwegian violin variant with extra sympathetic resonating strings
- **Vibe:** Mystical, Nordic
- **Use in a rap track:** Emotional hook melody
- **Real usage:** Norwegian folk tradition; no rap precedent
- **Video:** [Hardanger fiddle demo](https://www.youtube.com/results?search_query=hardanger+fiddle+demonstration)

### Cimbalom
- **Sound:** Large hammered dulcimer — like a piano made of metallic strings
- **Vibe:** Spy thriller, international crime, secret organizations ("John Wick" energy)
- **Use in a rap track:** Fast tremolo rolls or eerie arpeggios chopped into trap melodies
- **Real usage:** A go-to in modern spy/thriller film scoring; not established in rap, would read as fresh
- **Video:** [Cimbalom performance](https://www.youtube.com/results?search_query=cimbalom+performance)

### Accordion (minor key)
- **Sound:** Reedy, breathing chords
- **Vibe:** Italian mafia, street carnival darkness
- **Use in a rap track:** Under a "mafia trap" hook alongside muted trumpet
- **Real usage:** Occasionally sampled in mob/noir-themed rap production (common in beat-tape and drill-adjacent "mafioso rap" microtrends); no single iconic mainstream example confirmed
- **Video:** [Dark minor key accordion](https://www.youtube.com/results?search_query=dark+minor+key+accordion)

### Hang Drum (Handpan)
- **Sound:** Metallic hand percussion with rich, floating overtones
- **Vibe:** Meditative, introspective, expensive-sounding
- **Use in a rap track:** Melodic layer over sliding 808s in chill/lo-fi trap
- **Real usage:** Popular in lo-fi and chill-hop beat communities (YouTube/SoundCloud producer scene) though rarely credited on major-label rap records
- **Video:** [Handpan/hang drum performance](https://www.youtube.com/results?search_query=handpan+hang+drum+performance)

### Kalimba (Thumb Piano)
- **Sound:** Bright, plucked metal tines
- **Vibe:** Innocent but eerie; contrast tool
- **Use in a rap track:** High melodic accent, or a full lo-fi/chill-trap lead
- **Real usage:** A genuine trend in modern lo-fi hip-hop and chill trap beat packs (searchable across producer loop libraries); no major-label rap single is widely credited with a kalimba lead specifically
- **Video:** [Kalimba melody demo](https://www.youtube.com/results?search_query=kalimba+melody+demo)

### Jaw Harp
- **Sound:** Metallic twang, plucked against the teeth
- **Vibe:** Weird, playful, quirky-villain
- **Use in a rap track:** Rhythmic accent in western/outlaw-themed beats
- **Real usage:** Folk instrument worldwide; no rap precedent, purely a texture tool
- **Video:** [Jaw harp demo](https://www.youtube.com/results?search_query=jaw+harp+jew%27s+harp+demo)

### Frame Drum
- **Sound:** Deep, resonant hand drum
- **Vibe:** Ritualistic
- **Use in a rap track:** Sparse percussion layer under a verse
- **Real usage:** Ancient/folk percussion tradition; no rap precedent
- **Video:** [Frame drum performance](https://www.youtube.com/results?search_query=frame+drum+performance)

## 3. Electronic / Unusual-Pitch Instruments

### Theremin
- **Sound:** Continuous, hand-controlled pitch-bending electronic tone (played without touching the instrument)
- **Vibe:** Sci-fi horror, surreal, UFO menace
- **Use in a rap track:** Risers and transitions, or a spooky melodic line
- **Real usage:** **Confirmed in hip-hop** — Erykah Badu's *New Amerykah Part Two: Return of the Ankh* (2010) features theremin (played by Kirsten Agresta), on an album made with J Dilla, Questlove, and Madlib. More broadly famous in *Good Vibrations* (Beach Boys) and Led Zeppelin's *Whole Lotta Love*
- **Video:** [Theremin performance demo](https://www.youtube.com/results?search_query=theremin+performance+demo)

### Ondes Martenot
- **Sound:** An early (1920s) electronic instrument that glides between notes with human-like smoothness
- **Vibe:** Floating, vintage sci-fi, heavenly melancholy
- **Use in a rap track:** Sustained pad or lead
- **Real usage:** Famous in film scoring (used extensively by composer Jonny Greenwood and in classical works by Messiaen); not established in rap
- **Video:** [Ondes Martenot demo](https://www.youtube.com/results?search_query=ondes+martenot+demonstration)

## 4. Jazz & Lounge Instruments

### Rhodes Piano
- **Sound:** Warm electric piano
- **Vibe:** Late-night, expensive, jazzy, sophisticated
- **Use in a rap track:** Best used as the main chord bed underneath vocals
- **Real usage:** A hip-hop and neo-soul cornerstone — heard extensively across J Dilla's catalog, D'Angelo, and countless boom-bap/jazz-rap beats since the golden era; this is the most "already inside the genre" pick on this whole list
- **Video:** [Rhodes piano hip hop chords](https://www.youtube.com/results?search_query=rhodes+piano+hip+hop+chords+demo)

### Detuned Trumpet
- **Sound:** Vintage jazz horn pitched slightly off
- **Vibe:** 1940s crime film, drunken lounge, Italian mob
- **Use in a rap track:** Jazz loops and fills, especially layered with vinyl crackle
- **Real usage:** A defining texture of mafioso/noir-styled rap production (e.g., Raekwon/Ghostface-adjacent "cinematic mob rap" beats built on sampled horn loops); no single confirmed iconic example, but the aesthetic is well-established in that subgenre
- **Video:** [Muted jazz trumpet loop](https://www.youtube.com/results?search_query=muted+jazz+trumpet+loop+sample)

### Detuned Saxophone
- **Sound:** Smoky sax warped with tape wobble
- **Vibe:** Noir, melancholy, urban decay
- **Use in a rap track:** Hooks and outros
- **Real usage:** Common texture in lo-fi/jazz-rap beat culture; no single flagship mainstream example confirmed
- **Video:** [Detuned/warped saxophone loop](https://www.youtube.com/results?search_query=lofi+detuned+saxophone+loop)

## 5. Percussion & Foley ("Expensive & Dangerous")

These are less about melody and more about texture — layer them low in the mix for atmosphere, or use them as rhythmic replacements for standard hi-hats/snares.

| Sound | Effect | Best Placement |
|---|---|---|
| Anvil hits | Industrial crime-lord impact | Accent hit on a hard bar |
| Sword unsheathing scrape | Tension riser | Right before a drop |
| Pool balls cracking | Clean, sharp "street luxury" percussion | Hi-hat replacement |
| Dice roll / casino chips | Hustler ambience | Transition or intro |
| Zippo lighter flick | Subtle percussive detail | Snare ghost layer |
| Metal chain drag | Menacing texture | Intro |
| Car door slam | Percussive accent | Kick layer or transition |
| Spent shell casings on concrete | High-end texture | Hi-hat replacement |
| Leather glove creak | Subtle intimidation | Quiet verse texture |
| Bottle clink (w/ reverse reverb) | Mob lounge atmosphere | Transition |
| Gun-cocking / slide-rack / revolver spin | Percussive tension, no cliché gunshot | Rhythmic percussion in place of gunshot samples |

**Video reference:** [Foley sound library demo](https://www.youtube.com/results?search_query=cinematic+foley+sound+effects+library)

## 6. Vocal Effects

| Effect | How it hits | Placement |
|---|---|---|
| Deep pitch-dropped ad-libs | Use only on key words, not every bar | Punchlines |
| Reverse whispered ad-lib | Reverse a phrase directly into the snare/drop | Transitions |
| Radio dispatcher voice ("Unit 33, suspect moving…") | Sets a scene fast | Intro/skit |
| Police-scanner texture | Low-volume background atmosphere | Under a verse |
| Demonic formant shift | Lower formants carefully to avoid mud | Hook ad-libs |
| Gang chant, pitched down an octave | Drop tag or hook response | Hook |
| Breath-as-percussion | Chop breaths into rhythmic hits | Hi-hat layer |

## 7. Production Textures & Transitions

| Texture | Effect | Best Use |
|---|---|---|
| Reverse reverb | Audio swells into the hit | Before snares/vocals, or any instrument one-shot |
| Vinyl crackle | Dusty record noise | Entire intro |
| Tape stop | Beat briefly slows to a halt | Punchline transitions |
| Glitch reverb | Reverb chops and stutters | Beat switch |
| Half-time effect | Everything suddenly feels slower | Hook |
| Reverse 808 inhale | A reversed 808 fades in before the real hit | Right before the drop |

## 8. Ambience / Sound-Design Beds

Layer these **low in the mix** — the goal is atmosphere just below conscious perception, not a sound effect you notice directly.

| Sound | Vibe | Placement |
|---|---|---|
| Hydrophone (underwater mic) recordings | Aquatic, alien | Ambient intro |
| Geiger counter clicks | Tension, paranoia | Sparse percussion layer |
| EMF interference / electrical hum | Industrial, cyberpunk | Intro/transition |
| Contact-mic recordings (metal, glass, wood) | Mechanical, intimate | Percussive texture |
| Tape machine startup/shutdown | Vintage, nostalgic | Track open/close |
| Typewriter keys | Calculated, methodical | Hi-hat replacement |
| Ice cracking | Cold, dramatic | Beat drop |
| Large ship-hull groans | Massive, ominous | Background atmosphere |
| Subway tunnel ambience | Urban, lonely | Intro bed |
| Forest at night (insects, distant owls) | Isolated, suspenseful | Storytelling intro |
| Old film projector | Noir, nostalgic | Lo-fi boom-bap intro |
| Mechanical watch ticking | Luxury, inevitability | Quiet storytelling verse |
| Casino floor murmur / ice in a whiskey glass / distant siren / rain on windshield | "Rich criminal movie scene" | Layered low under a hook |

## 10. Sound Design Recipes

**Reverse 808 inhale** — Reverse a short 808, fade it in right before the real 808 hits. Makes the drop feel like it's sucking air out of the room.

**Gun-cocking rhythm without cliché gunshots** — Build percussion from metallic clicks, slide racks, revolver spins, and chamber snaps instead of gunshot samples.

**Luxury menace ambience bed** — Stack low-volume: casino floor murmur, elevator ding, ice in a whiskey glass, leather seat creak, distant siren, rain on a windshield, money-counter machine.

**Reverse reverb entrance** — Take any one-shot (koto, bassoon, duduk, piano, vocal ad-lib), add reverb, print it, reverse it, and place it directly before the dry hit. Instant cinematic villain entrance.

**Broken music box in the trap house** — Simple minor melody on music box, detune slightly, add vinyl wobble, layer an 808 underneath.
