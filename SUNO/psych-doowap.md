01. Jukebox After Midnight

• Vibe: Nocturnal, narcotic, catchy, sinister, vintage-futurist.
• Notable sounds / instruments: Tremolo guitar, upright piano, Rhodes, celesta, chopped male doo-wop vowels, tape delay, vinyl, clipped 808.

```text
126 BPM / 63 BPM half-time, psychedelic doo-wop trap built from a warped four-chord sock-hop progression. Use tremolo electric guitar, dusty upright piano, Rhodes, celesta, brushed snare ghosts, and a low male “doo / wah” harmony chopped into tiny instrumental syllables. Let the intro feel like a damaged 1958 jukebox before the 808 arrives. Drums are hardcore but spacious: clipped kick, dry clap-snare, swung hats, rim ghosts, short triplet bursts, and a thick tuned 808 with slow glides only at phrase endings. Reverse-reverb selected chord stabs into downbeats, smear one vocal harmony through tape delay, and use brief tape-chew dropouts before punchlines. Lead rap is cold, highly articulate, clever, medium-low, syncopated and slightly behind the beat, with compact multisyllabic rhymes and hard consonants.
```
